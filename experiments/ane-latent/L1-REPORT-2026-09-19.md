## 4. Evidence-contract hardening (implemented, tested)

Changes in `evidence.py`, covered by `tests/test_evidence_contract.py`:

- Real held-out coverage is required at each of S = 1, 8, 32; extra cases at another S do not substitute.
- Cases and windows require unique non-empty string ids; a duplicate or missing id invalidates the import.
- Each case carries `input_sha256`; each window carries `baseline_input_sha256` and `candidate_input_sha256`, which must be equal. `import_evidence` requires every such hash to name a file in `artifacts`, so identity rests on verified bytes.

## 5. Private fixed-prefix trace collector (implemented, no model launched)

`replay.py` provides `collect_trace(step, prompt_ids, forced_ids, vocab_size, label)`, `write_trace`, `read_trace` and `compare_traces`. The backend is an injected callable returning full logits; native, MLX and BF16 adapters are not yet written. Guarantees tested in `tests/test_replay.py`:

- The forced continuation is appended at every step regardless of the backend's argmax, so a candidate that disagrees from step 0 is still compared over the whole sequence on identical prefixes.
- Top-k or partial logits are refused; traces with different prompts, forced tokens or prefixes are refused.
- Nonfinite steps are recorded and make the comparison `invalid` rather than being dropped.
- On-disk traces are write-once and hash-checked on read.
- Gates applied: mean KL <= 0.001 nats/token and NLL increase <= 0.01 nats/token (PLAN.md).

## 6. Host-side packing (implemented, CPU emulation only)

`packing.py` packs `[S, K]` row-vector activations into `[1, K, 1, allocated_S]` fp16 surfaces, converts `[K, N]` weights to `[N, K, 1, 1]` blobs, and emulates the drafted 1x1 convolution. `tests/test_packing.py` covers transposed packing with asymmetric non-square values, zero vs sentinel padding leakage, and the two-stage A/B layout.
